Creating a safer, smarter, and more compassionate world for our furry companions.

📖 About the Pet Amicus

Pet Amicus is a smart pet care ecosystem designed to provide a safe, hygienic, and comfortable environment for pets while reducing the burden on pet owners. It integrates IoT devices, intelligent automation, a mobile application, and selected AI-powered features to ensure continuous monitoring, personalized care, and improved pet well-being.

The system addresses common challenges faced by pet owners, such as irregular feeding, lack of supervision, separation anxiety, hygiene maintenance, and environmental control. It also has the potential to be adapted as a smart shelter solution for stray animals through collaboration with NGOs and municipal authorities.

Rather than replacing human care, Pet Amicus acts as a smart companion that assists owners in providing better care through technology.

🎯 Objectives

Improve pet health and well-being.
Assist owners in providing continuous care.
Reduce stress and anxiety for pets left alone.
Maintain a clean and comfortable living environment.
Promote responsible pet ownership.
Support future smart shelter systems for stray animals.

🚨 The Problem

Modern lifestyles often leave pets alone for several hours every day. During this time they may experience:

Missed meals and irregular hydration
Extreme temperatures
Poor hygiene
Lack of supervision
Emotional stress and loneliness

Similarly, stray animals struggle with inadequate shelter, food, hygiene, and safety.

Most existing products solve only one of these issues. Pet Amicus combines multiple solutions into one integrated system.
